Ratios

      Meter is what you’d use on set: each light’s incident reading on its own, meter facing the light (the background on the wall, facing camera). Two lights at the same level are 1:1, and every stop doubles the light, so 1 stop = 2:1. The ratio buttons set the fill by level. Switch to Spot to see what the camera records on the frame.

      Spot compares reflected readings at the spots on the frame, so it takes in skin and wardrobe tone, surface angle and spill: a rim on a dark jacket reads lower than the light hitting it. Key : Fill is K spot against F spot, the two cheeks. In this mode the ratio buttons set the fill so the face reads that ratio. Drag a spot to measure somewhere else.

      Room bounce adds the light each lamp would bounce back off a real room. Leave it off to see each light on its own; switch it on for a truer picture of how dark the shadows will get. The camera settings don’t change the light, only how it’s recorded: the ratios stay the same. Spots and false colour read the frame after exposure.
