Display

Blur in the camera picture

The blur strength only changes the camera picture. The prism, colours and numbers always use the real optics. "In focus" means the blur spot is no bigger than the circle of confusion: about 1/1500 of the sensor diagonal (Standard) or 1/3000 (Critical, for big screens and 4K). The colour fades to blue by 3× that size.
