The day on set. Every department converges, the shot cycle turns, and the story is captured frame by frame.

The work you do before you arrive on set determines the quality of the work you do when you get there. This section is not a checklist with deadlines: it is the intellectual and practical preparation that should inform everything in the pipeline below. A crew member who has done this research knows not just what to do, but why, and that understanding is what separates a technically competent operator from a genuinely valuable collaborator.
