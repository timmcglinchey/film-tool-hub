Pipeline

The footage exists. Now the film gets made: shaped, coloured, and sculpted in the edit until the story finally reveals itself.

Post-production is not purely technical. Every cut, grade, and sound choice carries meaning, and the best practitioners make those choices intentionally, informed by the history and language of the craft. Work through the three areas below before you begin. Return to them as you go.
