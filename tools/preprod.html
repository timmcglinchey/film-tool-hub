Before pre-production begins in earnest, every crew member should invest time in understanding their role as thoroughly as possible. This is not a production phase — it has no deadline or deliverable in the traditional sense. It is academic groundwork: the research, reading, and conceptual preparation that will make everything that follows more informed and more intentional.
